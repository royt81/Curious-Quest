🦊 Privacy policy

Curious Quest – free games for curious kids · Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Curious Quest does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking — on the website and in the app.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings (sound, colours, chosen world) and high scores are saved only on your own device, in the browser's local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • You can delete them at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.
  • The game does not use cookies.

Reading voice

Spoken questions use the speech voice built into your device. Nothing is recorded, and the microphone is never used.

No third parties

The game makes no requests to other companies: fonts, pictures and sounds are all delivered with the game itself. There is no advertising and no analytics software.

Hosting

The game is delivered by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Like every web server, Cloudflare briefly processes technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not have access to this data and do not store it.

Children

Curious Quest is made for children. Because no personal data is collected at all, no data from children is collected either.

Your rights

You have the right to information, correction, deletion and restriction of processing, and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. For any questions, contact roytate.berlin@gmail.com.
