Snakes & Ladders — 5 Flat Geometric Board Styles

Switch themes to check every style still reads well across all 5 palettes. Green = ladder, Red = snake.

1 · Bold Checker

Classic alternating checkerboard with bold flat number badges, triangle corner markers for ladder feet / snake heads, and thick flat-color ladder rails & snake bodies.

2 · Pathway Trail

A single flat ribbon traces the full 1→100 route through tinted progress zones, so kids can literally see the path. Ladders are solid bridges, snakes are bold tapered trails.

3 · Bauhaus Corners

Minimalist geometric style: every cell gets a flat triangular corner accent, ladders become thin rail-and-rung circuits, and snakes are a chain of shrinking flat circles.

4 · Zone Bands

Horizontal "race track" bands group each row of 10 into a flat color zone, with circular number badges. Ladders are flat trapezoid ramps, snakes are chevron arrows pointing back to their head.

5 · Diagonal Split

Every tile is split into two flat triangles for a herringbone texture, with diamond number badges. Ladders are angled flat ramps, snakes are a chain of rotated flat squares — a friendly "caterpillar".
